First real drum substem experiment

128 BPM, four bars, 4/4. Eight solos and seven cumulative mixes. Native stereo 44.1 kHz / 16-bit inputs; originals unchanged.

Three models trained on bars 1–3, 300 updates each. Bar 4 was used for development comparisons, including choosing a finer wavelet configuration. This is one shared performance, not an independent test set. The master limiter means solo sums are not exact mixture targets. Reference masks use the target solos and are not a blind separator.

What this establishes

The real audio runs through verified loading, native-rate hybrid encoding, supervised learning, waveform rendering and solo-reference loss measurement. It does not yet deliver clean eight-way separation, blind drum classification, exact MIDI velocity recovery or automatic identification of synthesizer versus sampled origins.
